FAQ

Questions, answered

Where does the AI run? Is my data sent to OpenAI?

No. ClaimPaid AI runs its language model on our own servers. Your documents are chunked, embedded, and queried entirely inside our infrastructure — nothing is sent to OpenAI, Google, or any third-party AI API. That's the whole point.

What documents can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), plain text, Markdown, and CSV — plus direct import from any public URL. Payer policy PDFs work great.

Will it hallucinate?

It's grounded: answers are written from retrieved passages, and every factual claim carries a citation you can click to verify. When your documents don't contain the answer, it says so instead of inventing one.

Do I need a separate login from ClaimPaid RCM?

No — one ClaimPaid account works for both. If you're already signed into the RCM app, the AI app just works.

Can I use it on my website?

Yes — paste one script tag and you get a floating chat bubble powered by your knowledge base. Or use the REST API to build it into your own product.
