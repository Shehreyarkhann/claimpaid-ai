FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data used to train answers for other customers?

No. Every workspace is isolated: your documents are searchable only inside your workspace, and anything ClaimPaid learns from your work — coding decisions, decided appeals — applies only to your workspace. Data is never pooled across customers.

What documents can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), plain text, Markdown, and CSV — plus direct import from any public URL. Payer policy PDFs work great.

Will it hallucinate?

It's grounded: answers are written from retrieved passages, and every factual claim carries a citation you can click to verify. When your documents don't contain the answer, it says so instead of inventing one. The coding assistant follows the same rule — a code is Valid only when your note supports it, quoted back to you; otherwise it's Review or an outright Abstain.

Do I need a separate login from ClaimPaid RCM?

No — one ClaimPaid account works for both. If you're already signed into the RCM app, the AI app just works.

Can I use it on my website?

Yes — paste one script tag and you get a floating chat bubble powered by your knowledge base. Or use the REST API to build it into your own product.
