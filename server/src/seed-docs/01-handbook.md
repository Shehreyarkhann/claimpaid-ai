# Northwind Support Handbook

## Getting started
Welcome to Northwind Cloud Storage. To create an account, visit app.northwind.example.com and click Sign Up. You can sign up with an email address or with Google single sign-on. New accounts start on the 14-day Pro trial automatically — no credit card required.

## Plans and pricing
Northwind offers three plans: Starter at $12 per user per month (100 GB storage), Pro at $29 per user per month (1 TB storage, priority support), and Enterprise with custom pricing (unlimited storage, SSO/SAML, dedicated success manager, 99.99% uptime SLA). Annual billing saves 20% on Starter and Pro.

## Storage limits
Individual files can be up to 50 GB on Pro and Enterprise, and up to 5 GB on Starter. If you hit your storage quota, uploads pause until you free space or upgrade. Admins get an email alert at 80% and 95% of quota.

## Security
All files are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Enterprise plans add SSO/SAML, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, and data residency choices (US or EU). Northwind is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

## Support hours
Standard support (Starter/Pro) is available Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm Eastern, with a 4-hour first-response target on Pro. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support with a 30-minute response target for critical issues.
