# Returns, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

## Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime; you keep access until the end of the billing period and are not charged again. Annual plans cancelled within the first 30 days receive a full refund. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded pro-rata for unused full months, minus a 10% processing fee.

## How to cancel
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Cancellation takes effect immediately for the renewal — your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation in case you return.

## Enterprise contracts
Enterprise agreements have a 12-month minimum term. Early termination requires 60 days written notice and incurs 50% of the remaining contract value.

## Chargebacks
If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of contacting us first, your account is suspended until the dispute is resolved. Please email billing@northwind.example.com — most billing issues are resolved within one business day.

## Data export
You can export all your files at any time from Settings → Export. Exports are provided as a ZIP archive and include file metadata. There is no fee for data export, ever.
