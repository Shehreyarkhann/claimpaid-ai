## Medicare's 5-Level Appeal Process

Medicare has a formal 5-level system. Most billing operations handle levels 1–2:

1. **Redetermination** (MAC) — 120 days from remittance. Decision in 60 days.
2. **Reconsideration** (QIC) — 180 days from redetermination decision.
3. **ALJ Hearing** — 60 days from reconsideration. Amount in controversy must meet threshold.
4. **Medicare Appeals Council** — 60 days from ALJ decision.
5. **Federal District Court** — 60 days from Council decision.

## Writing an Effective Appeal Letter

Every appeal letter needs these elements:

1. **Patient and claim identifiers** — name, DOB, member ID, claim number, dates of service.
2. **The denial reason** — quote the exact CARC/RARC codes from the remittance.
3. **Your counter-argument** — address the specific reason, not a generic complaint.
4. **Supporting documentation** — medical records, prior authorization numbers, timely-filing proof.
5. **The specific ask** — "We request reconsideration and payment of $X."
6. **A deadline reference** — "This appeal is submitted within the 180-day window."

## Appeal Letter Templates by Denial Type

ClaimPaid RCM includes auto-generated appeal letters for 8 denial categories:
- Medical necessity — attach clinical notes showing the service met medical necessity criteria
- Prior authorization missing — include the auth number if obtained, or document medical urgency
- Timely filing — only appealable with proof of timely submission (clearinghouse reports)
- Coordination of benefits — include the primary payer's EOB
- Coding errors — submit a corrected claim with the right CPT/modifiers, reference the original
- Duplicate claim — verify it is truly a duplicate before writing off
- Patient demographics — correct and resubmit; appeal if the payer has the wrong data on file
- Non-covered service — check for ABN on file (Medicare); appeal with medical necessity documentation

## Follow-Up Cadence

- **Open appeals**: follow up every 3 days
- **In-progress appeals**: follow up every 7 days
- **Submitted appeals**: follow up every 14 days
- **Stale 21+ days with no movement**: escalate to high priority immediately
