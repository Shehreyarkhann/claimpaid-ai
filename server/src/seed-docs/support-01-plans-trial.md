# ClaimPaid Plans and Free Trial

## Plans

ClaimPaid has four plans:

- **Starter — $199/month.** 1,000 claims per month, 3 team seats. RCM app only (no AI product).
- **Pro + AI — $499/month.** 10,000 claims per month, 15 team seats, AI product included with 2,000 AI queries per month.
- **AI Only — $99/month.** Standalone AI product: 1,000 AI queries per month, 5 seats. No RCM app.
- **Enterprise — custom pricing.** Unlimited claims, seats, and AI queries.

## Free trial

Every plan starts with a **30-day free trial — no credit card required**. You pick a plan during the 5-step onboarding wizard (Welcome → Workspace → Goals → Plan → Success) and the trial starts immediately.

During the trial you get the full limits of the plan you picked. If the trial expires without payment, paid features lock behind a paywall — **your data is safe** and you can reactivate any time by adding a payment method.

## Usage limits

Claims and seats are metered per calendar month against your plan. At 80% of a limit you see a warning banner; at 100% the action is blocked with an upgrade prompt (for example, creating a claim returns a "claim limit" notice, inviting a teammate returns a "seat limit" notice). Usage bars in the Billing tab show claims, seats, and AI queries used versus your plan. Enterprise plans are unlimited.
