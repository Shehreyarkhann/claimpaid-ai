# ClaimPaid Billing

## Billing tab

The Billing tab shows your plan status card (Free trial with days left, Active, or Trial ended), a trial countdown bar, and buttons to add a payment method or manage billing. It also lists your last 12 invoices.

## Paying

Payments are processed through Stripe. When your trial still has days left, billing is scheduled to start exactly when the trial ends — you are never double-charged for trial days. If your trial already expired, the first charge happens immediately.

**Before checkout you must review and e-sign the Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** in the app. Checkout is blocked until the BAA is signed.

## Cancelling

Go to Billing → Manage billing to cancel. Your data stays in place and you can reactivate any time. There are no lock-ins and no cancellation fees.

## Invoices

Past invoices are listed in the Billing tab. For invoice or payment questions, email support@claimpaid.io — we reply within one business day.
