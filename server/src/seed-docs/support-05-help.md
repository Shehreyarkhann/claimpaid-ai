# ClaimPaid Help and Troubleshooting

## Contacting support

Email **support@claimpaid.io** or open a ticket from the in-app Support page. **We reply within one business day.**

## Login trouble

Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then contact support@claimpaid.io. After 5 failed login attempts the account locks for 15 minutes and clears on its own.

## Google sign-in

Click "Continue with Google" on the login page. If you get a token error, make sure you are signing in with the same email address on your ClaimPaid account, then try again or contact support.

## Importing existing claims

Go to Claims → Import and download the CSV template so the columns line up. Imported claims run through the 15-rule scrubber before they reach the worklist.

## Learning resources

The ClaimPaid blog at claimpaid.io/blog/ has 28+ in-depth guides on denials, timely filing, EDI, and practice operations.

## Cancelling

Billing → Manage billing → cancel. Data is retained and you can reactivate any time. No fees, no lock-in.
